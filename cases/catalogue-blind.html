Case catalogue (blind)

72 cases in 9 families. Generated by generators/catalogue.py from the case files; do not edit. Lessons, verdicts and reasons are left out.

A verdict is relative to how the vocabulary is read and to the declarations in force.

  • Compatible: Whatever the vocabulary leaves open, some use meets both policies.
  • Incompatible: Whatever the vocabulary leaves open, no use meets both policies.
  • Unknown: It depends on something the vocabulary leaves open, and the reason names it.
  • Rejected: the policy uses an operator that does not apply to this kind of value, or a value that names no entry of the vocabulary.
  • Accepted: a control. The condition fits this kind of value, and the policy goes on to a verdict.
  • Every use has at least one value for each condition, for example at least one language.
  • If each of two values lies below the other, they are the same value.
  • For places, 'Use in France only' means the place given for the use is France itself, not a place within France.
