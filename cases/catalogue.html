The three sorts say what a resource supplies. nom: names only, so two values are the same or they are not. tax: kinds, so one value can be a narrower kind of another. mer: places and their parts, so one value can lie within another.

A verdict is relative to a binding and to the declarations in force. Compatible: a use satisfies both policies in every structure. Incompatible: in none. Unknown: in some. A case can also be rejected before any verdict, because an operator is ill sorted at the operand’s sort or a value names no concept of the slice.
